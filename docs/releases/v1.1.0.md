# ZEN Manager 1.1.0 — Balance

Playlists.

## New: the Playlists screen

Make and manage playlists on the player, without the player.

- **See the playlists already on your Zen**, including ones made on the device
  itself, with their track counts.
- **Create** a playlist, name it, and fill it.
- **Add tracks** from a window showing everything on the player as artists and
  albums. Tick a whole artist or album to add all of it at once.
- **Reorder** tracks by dragging them, or with the up and down buttons.
- **Remove** tracks, **rename** the playlist, or **delete** it.

Nothing is written to the player until you press Save, so you can rearrange
freely and discard if you change your mind.

## Also in this release

- Disabled buttons no longer look like empty outlines.
- macOS downloads now carry the version in the filename, matching Linux.
- Corrected the Linux install instructions: `libmtp-runtime` is the package to
  install, and it pulls in the right library. The old instructions named
  `libmtp9`, which does not exist on Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13 — it was renamed
  `libmtp9t64` there.

## Requirements

Unchanged: macOS 11 or newer, or Linux (x86_64 or arm64) with libmtp.
