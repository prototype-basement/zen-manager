# ZEN Manager 1.0.0 — Awakening

The first release. ZEN Manager puts music on a Creative Zen Micro from a modern
Mac or Linux machine — no Windows, no internet, no accounts, no telemetry.

## What it does

- **Drop files or whole folders** onto the window and transfer them in one go.
- **Your tags come with them.** Artist, album, title and track number are read
  from your files and written to the player, so tracks land under the right
  artist and album in the Zen's own menus instead of as bare filenames.
- **Problems are caught before you transfer**, not halfway through: formats the
  player can't play, tracks already on it, files that won't fit, and files with
  no tags are all flagged in the queue.
- **Check a file's tags** before sending it.
- **Browse what's on the player** by artist and album, and delete whole artists,
  albums or single tracks.
- **See the device's battery and storage** at a glance.
- **Light and dark themes**, or follow your system.
- **English and Croatian**, with translations easy to contribute.

## Requirements

- **macOS 11 or newer**, Apple Silicon or Intel. Nothing else to install.
- **Linux**, x86_64 or arm64, glibc 2.35 or newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 and
  later), with libmtp from your distribution.

Tested on a Creative Zen Micro. Other MTP players may work but are untested.

## Known limitations

- Plug the player in before opening the app, or press Refresh — it doesn't
  notice a device being connected.
- Error messages from the device layer are still in English.
- Right-to-left languages display correctly, but the layout isn't mirrored.

ZEN Manager is a transfer tool, not a tag editor: tag your library with
something built for the job — MusicBrainz Picard, Mp3tag, Kid3 — then transfer.
